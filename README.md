# ⚡ ELECTROFIK

**Strategic Fleet Electrification Simulator**

ELECTROFIK is a digital platform designed to help companies make strategic decisions about the electrification of their vehicle fleets. It transforms complex data into clear, visual information for quick assessment of whether switching from combustion-engine to electric vehicles is feasible, cost-effective, and operationally viable.

## Features

- **Fleet Simulator** — Enter your company's fleet data (size, vehicle type, daily mileage, fuel type) and get instant analysis
- **Cost Comparison** — Compare actual cost per kilometer between combustion and electric vehicles, including fuel, energy, maintenance, and insurance
- **Savings Calculator** — Calculate monthly and annual savings from fleet electrification
- **Environmental Impact** — Measure CO₂ reduction and equivalent trees planted
- **Fleet Utilization** — Assess how well electric vehicles fit your daily operational needs
- **Feasibility Score** — Get an automated feasibility assessment based on your use case
- **Company Comparison** — Save multiple company profiles and compare them side by side with radar charts and detailed tables

## Tech Stack

- **React** (Vite) — Fast, modern frontend
- **Recharts** — Interactive charts and data visualization
- **CSS** — Custom responsive design, no external CSS framework

## How It Works

1. Enter your company name, fleet size, vehicle type, daily distance, and energy prices
2. Click "Calculate Results" to generate a comprehensive analysis
3. Review cost breakdowns, energy consumption, CO₂ savings, and feasibility
4. Save results and run simulations for other companies
5. Compare multiple companies side by side when 2+ are saved
